Meet our SmartPass

A hall pass system we built from scratch: students start a pass in a couple of taps, and teachers see everything as it happens.

  • Digital hall passes with a countdown timer
  • A teacher dashboard with a live hall monitor
  • Pass limits, requests and emergency mode
  • Analytics to see how passes get used

How it works

Three taps, zero paper

From the classroom to the hallway and back again.

1

Start a pass

Pick a destination and tap go. The timer starts right away.

2

Teachers see it live

The hall monitor shows who is out, where they went and for how long.

3

End the pass

Back in the room, one tap ends it and the numbers feed the analytics.
